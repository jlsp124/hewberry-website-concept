### Existing web inventory

The apex and HTTP variants redirected to `https://www.hewberryalarms.ca/`. The homepage, logo, styles, contact and article HTML were reachable with the CLI even when the search reader returned an access error. The site runs WordPress with Avada and WooCommerce. Capture and inspection used a 1440 px desktop and a 390 px mobile browser. Home starts with office, account and cart chrome, then a Work Sans headline on a dark stock photograph. The published shield combines blue with red signal arcs, and the current interface uses amber action accents.

The CLI inventory followed all 83 distinct URLs across the site's 13 public sitemap families, reviewing public headings and template content. Access used read-only requests.

| Route family | Observation and implication |
| --- | --- |
| Home and contact | Services and trust information live mainly on the homepage. Contact publishes office details and a non-emergency form. Bring the service routes, support and quote action forward on both screen sizes. |
| Benefits article | This is visible in the main navigation. It mixes general advice with older deterrence and insurance claims. Preserve its inbound intent in production with a relevant redirect or updated article following editorial review. |
| Services, work, blog, sample page | Indexed older theme pages have generic electrician content, placeholder text or numbers. Their presence makes the main offering harder to verify from search. Retain only validated facts. |
| Shop, products, account, cart, checkout | A live public catalogue and an account entry establish that the parts flow exists today. Checkout reached the empty cart without a purchase. Transaction volume, inventory freshness and integration requirements remain unconfirmed. The concept points parts users to Hewberry’s existing shop. |
| Posts, portfolios, FAQs and taxonomies | Public indexes also contain theme posts and portfolio examples, an FAQ archive, and category, tag and author pages. Template copy and image galleries are weak evidence of completed work. Map worthwhile inbound URLs and omit duplicate archives after reviewing traffic and search data for a production migration. |

The homepage gallery uses filenames with an iStock prefix. Public availability alone does not grant reuse of those photographs. The demo uses the requested published Hewberry logo and separately licensed photography. ## Design research

This is a qualitative design review. Live HTML, case-study material and viewport snapshots support specific observations. An award listing or a screenshot cannot establish accessibility or field performance.

| Reference | Technique observed and decision |
| --- | --- |
| [Ivy Energy](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/ivy-energy/47839/) | The CSSDA case frames a complicated service with one central product image and a clear business context. Carry one dominant image into the Hewberry opening and make the offer legible in one sentence. |
| [Enpal case study](https://www.valentingerhard.com/work/enpal/) and [current live site](https://www.enpal.de/) | The case shows an architectural object communicating a technical household upgrade. The live mobile screenshot places qualification near the opening while stacking the main offer in one reading order. Translate that into property types and a quote path users can find early. |
| [Q-Industrial](https://www.q-industrial.com/) | A dark, high-contrast opening frames one glowing industrial object and a compact direct CTA. Transfer the focal discipline and warm/cool contrast with a real photograph and a simpler heading. |
| [Igloo Inc.](https://www.igloo.inc/) and [production case study](https://www.awwwards.com/igloo-inc-case-study.html) | The animated world has consistent material, light and camera behavior. Retain comparable consistency across still photography and a short signal sequence. Full WebGL would bring extra asset and interaction costs to the service task. |
| [ERA by Vide Infra](https://videinfra.com/work/era) | Architectural depth and trailing lines do storytelling work in the WebGL scene. A single abstract line in the Hewberry monitoring section can communicate passage from property to operator to response. |
| [Iventions](https://iventions.com/) | Large project imagery and selected proof let services sit in context. Hewberry’s eventual gallery should feature approved actual work with a specific problem and outcome. |
| [Why Zero](https://why.zero.university/) and [studio case study](https://wehatevanilla.com/work/why-zero-website/) | A continuous immersive scene heightens atmosphere. Screen readers, no-script visitors, and customers in a hurry need direct routes in a local service site. Keep scrolling voluntary and ordinary. |
| [Altieri](https://www.altieri.llc/) | Images fill oversized brand letters over white and communicate built-environment expertise before detail. Establish one clear photographic frame and preserve the established business name and logo. |
| [Custom Alarm case study](https://brandhoot.com/work/custom-alarm/) and [live site](https://www.custom-alarm.com/) | The mobile review showed an explicit residential/commercial choice and conspicuous account support. This is the strongest conversion reference. Hewberry gives support its own primary navigation link and carries service intent into the quote. |
| [Ajax Systems](https://ajax.systems/) | Manufacturer pages describe an ecosystem with distinct product categories and device imagery. Use a similar plain-language category structure at the service level and leave model selection to the installer conversation. |
| [Linear](https://linear.app/) | Its restrained type, colour and link hierarchy prompted a disciplined navigation and utilities pass. The first reduced-motion screenshot loaded the header before the hero, so detailed appearance observations were limited. |
| [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/), [Landing Love](https://www.landing.love/), [Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/) and [mesh3d](https://mesh3d.gallery/) | These provide discovery trails for motion and strong single-scene compositions. Derive mechanisms from credited examples and use original composition and content. |
| [Sanders Green Architecture](https://www.sandersgreen.ca/) | The independent Penticton reference grounds architecture in materials, slope and vegetation. It reinforced choosing a correctly identified landscape photograph and real material-rich property examples. |

The requester’s Design Intelligence library informed the process. Starting with the existing brand led to Work Sans, amber and coal. Native disclosure informed the service and FAQ patterns. Keeping guidance beside the inputs informed the quote’s validation and retry. The project reuses those principles and includes its own implementation.

### Technical and media choices

- Astro produces portable HTML and route metadata. A few native TypeScript enhancements handle the menu, disclosure photography, the quote and monitoring motion. The site has no client framework runtime. Astro, Playwright, the Axe integration and Lighthouse were checked against their package metadata and official documentation. [Astro static build](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/deploy/github/), [Playwright emulation](https://playwright.dev/docs/emulation), [axe-core](https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core), [Lighthouse](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/lighthouse/overview/).
- A bespoke 3D property could be built in Blender with CC0 HDRIs and materials from Poly Haven, delivered as compressed glTF through Three.js or React Three Fiber, and animated with native timelines or GSAP. A rendered image sequence would simplify GPU compatibility but would increase media transfer and cropping work. No candidate local property model with the desired visual quality plus the necessary verified rights was selected. CSS depth applied to a responsive still can preserve the site’s immediate reading order. [Poly Haven licence](https://polyhaven.com/license), [Poly Haven Pine Forest](https://polyhaven.com/collections/pine_forest), [Blender glTF](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/addons/import_export/scene_gltf2.html), [Three.js glTF loader](https://threejs.org/docs/#examples/en/loaders/GLTFLoader), [React Three Fiber](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/getting-started/introduction), [GSAP ScrollTrigger](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/), [CSS scroll animation timelines](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Guides/Scroll-driven_animations/Timelines).

## Production decisions

- Verify company ownership, current credentials, approved geographic coverage, equipment lines, support routing and the exact role of Prestige for new and existing accounts.
- Ask for approved images of real work, ideally one residential, one commercial and one construction project with permission to name the relevant context. Keep location details, equipment placement and customer identities private unless explicitly cleared.
- Decide whether component ordering is still actively used. Review sales, inventory, payment, login and support needs separately from marketing before migrating WooCommerce.
- Replace the simulation only after Hewberry approves who receives quotes, what data is collected, how it is retained, and how privacy is communicated. Preserve a direct phone path.
- Preserve search equity through a confirmed route map, production canonical and index settings, accessible templates and a review of location and service copy before launch.
